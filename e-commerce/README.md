# E-commerce Skills

Product selection, pricing, tracking, compliance research, and research-led product content production through AIsa.

<!-- category:start -->

This category contains 4 self-contained Agent Skills.

| Skill | Version | Description |
|---|---|---|
| [compliance-checker](./compliance-checker/) | 1.0.0 | Check cross-border selling requirements for a product, destination, planned selling period and marketplace. Use when a seller asks what they need before selling overseas, which documents or labels are missing, or whether listing claims match their evidence. Start with 'I want to sell X to Y' and progressively clarify critical details; research official regulations and platform policies through AIsa. |
| [e-commerce-content-studio](./e-commerce-content-studio/) | 1.1.0 | Create platform-specific product images, localized listing copy and product videos with AIsa. Use for 商品图、商品文案 and listing content for Amazon US, TikTok Shop US or Shopify product pages. Requires real product photos, current platform research and a user-approved creative playbook; offers suitable models from the live AIsa catalog. |
| [product-selection](./product-selection/) | 1.0.2 | Select products to sell, compare suitable destination markets, and calculate viable prices for sellers. Use for product selection, product-market fit, marketplace assortment research, competitor price comparisons and landed-cost pricing. Supports sellers with no starting preference, a chosen market, or a chosen product; uses AIsa MCP for live evidence and TypeScript helpers for ranking and pricing. |
| [product-tracking](./product-tracking/) | 1.0.1 | Track a specified product, competitor SKU or ASIN over time: product prices, availability, ratings and public demand signals. Use for product watchlists, competitor price monitoring and changes since a previous observation. Amazon US is supported through AIsa DataForSEO with persistent local snapshots. Seller-level information is not supported. |
