# Coverage and applicability starting points

This file provides routing guidance and official-source starting points reviewed on **2026-11-03**. It is not a complete legal checklist, a cached statement of current law or evidence that a particular product complies. Research the relevant rules and their status for every assessment using [research.md](research.md).

## Guided coverage and on-demand research

| Area | Initial guided coverage | How to handle other requests |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jurisdictions | US federal requirements; EU baseline with Germany national starting points | Research other countries and US states from their official sources when requested or materially implicated. Mark this as on-demand research, state what was checked, and keep unverified national/local requirements visible. Do not reject a market just because it has no maintained starting pack. |
| Platforms | Amazon US, Amazon Germany, TikTok Shop US; Shopify sales targeting US/Germany | Research the specified platform/site's official policy and review supplied content. Do not claim a tested listing adapter, account access or complete policy coverage for another platform. |
| Products | Ordinary adult apparel, bags, stationery and simple non-electrical home accessories | Regulated categories receive requirements/document triage and targeted research. Identify laboratory, specialist or authoritative lookup work that remains; do not reject the whole task or claim certification. |
| Workflows | Launch preparation, listing/claim review, supplied-document gaps and notice triage | Produce findings and preparation materials. Filing registrations, submitting appeals, responding to claimants, paying duties and publishing listings are not automatic outputs of an assessment. |

Great Britain, Northern Ireland, deeper US state modules and additional EU national modules are future maintained packs. They remain available for explicitly bounded on-demand research. Keep Great Britain and Northern Ireland separate when their product rules differ. EU-wide rules do not automatically settle member-state tax, packaging, language or enforcement requirements.

Marketplace region is not the sole jurisdiction. Determine destination countries, seller establishment, origin, import route and inventory locations when relevant. Shopify does not supply a destination by itself. A marketplace collecting a tax does not automatically eliminate other seller registrations or obligations.

## Product and issue branches

Select only branches relevant to the user's facts. Start with the useful ones while collecting material unknowns; do not ask every seller every question.

| Trigger | Facts to establish | Research and review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Any consumer product | Intended use, users, materials, model/variant, responsible seller/importer and destination | Applicable safety framework, traceability/labels, instructions/warnings, incident/recall obligations and available supporting documents. |
| Children or toy-like presentation | Intended age, actual presentation and foreseeable use | Children's-product/toy scope, testing and certification, labeling. Do not accept an “adult only” statement as conclusive when imagery or marketing contradicts it. |
| Electrical, rechargeable or wireless | Power source, battery chemistry/capacity, charger, radio functions, shipping mode | Product and electrical safety, radio/EMC where relevant, batteries, transport and end-of-life duties. Distinguish transport test documents from product conformity evidence. |
| Food contact, food, cosmetics or medical claims | Contact materials/use, ingredients, intended effects, marketing and destination | Relevant specialist regulator/category rules. Do not extend general consumer-product guidance to these without checking exclusions and sector rules. |
| Textile, packaging, chemical or environmental features | Materials, composition, packaging configuration, branding and responsible parties | Category labeling, chemical restrictions, packaging/EPR responsibilities and evidence for environmental claims. |
| Health, safety, performance, environmental or origin claim | Exact wording, implied meaning and evidence actually held | Applicable advertising standard, necessary substantiation and supported wording. Removing a risky phrase does not establish the underlying product is compliant. |
| Imported goods | Detailed composition/function, origin, value, ship-from, import date, importer and fulfilment | Classification candidates, current tariff measures/restrictions, customs documents and responsibility. Keep VAT/sales tax and customs duty analyses distinct. |
| Branding, distinctive design or copied content | Marks, model/design identifiers, owner/licence information, exact assets | Preliminary IP research and relevant official records. No-match results are not clearance; patent/design questions may need specialist analysis. |
| Own-store sales or policy review | Destination, B2C/B2B, checkout, returns terms, data/cookie practices | Pre-contract disclosures, withdrawal/guarantees, privacy and platform terms. State unreviewed national/state overlays. |
| Complaint, takedown or legal notice | Actual notice, sender, identifiers, jurisdiction, received date, stated deadline and requested action | Separate allegations from established facts; extract explicit deadlines accurately; identify missing context and specialist handoff needs. Do not invent a deadline, assume proper service, or contact a sender automatically. |

## Official sources to start from

Read the relevant page and follow its current links to the controlling instrument, category guidance or official lookup. A portal link alone does not support a finding. Login screens and JavaScript-only pages are access limitations, not verified policy content.

### US

- [CPSC online-seller FAQ](https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/Online-Sellers-Safety-Guide-FAQs): route children's/general-use classification and certificate questions. Keep a supplier test report separate from a manufacturer's/importer's certificate; inspect which rules apply before listing required evidence.
- [CPSC recalls and safety warnings](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls): investigate exact model/brand matches and the record's scope. No match is not a product-safety finding.
- [FTC health-product claims guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance): review substantiation and express/implied claims. It is agency guidance, not itself a statute or safe harbor.
- [USPTO clearance-search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks): establish the limits of exact-name checks and public web searching. Follow relevant registry records; distinguish trademarks from patents/designs/copyright.
- [USITC tariff schedule](https://hts.usitc.gov/) and [CBP CROSS](https://rulings.cbp.gov/): investigate current classification and analogous rulings, including modifications/revocations. Recheck origin-specific measures and entry-date rules; do not reuse historical low-value thresholds or assume a ruling on a different item binds the user's import.
- For specialist categories, discover the relevant official regulator and current guidance, such as FDA for food/cosmetics/medical-product scope or FCC for radio equipment. A category name alone does not prove an agency's jurisdiction.

### EU baseline and Germany

- [Commission product-safety portal](https://commission.europa.eu/topics/business-and-industry/product-safety_en) and [GPSR official summary](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/EN/legal-content/summary/general-product-safety-regulation-2023.html): investigate scope, responsible economic operators, distance-sale information and Safety Gate. Follow the regulation and its applicable provisions; distinguish manufacturer, importer, authorised representative and marketplace duties.
- [EU CE guidance](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/product-rules-compliance/general-product-compliance/ce-marking/): determine whether a covered sector requires CE marking before requesting it. Do not require CE for every consumer product or describe a logo as proof of conformity.
- [Commission VAT One Stop Shop](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/one-stop-shop_en): assess the relevant VAT scheme, establishment, stock location and marketplace role. IOSS eligibility is a VAT question and must not be treated as a customs-duty exemption.
- [EU distance-selling guidance](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm): distinguish withdrawal, defects/legal guarantees and voluntary returns; check product exceptions and national implementation.
- [German packaging authority: online retailers](https://www.verpackungsregister.org/en/knowledge-bases/mail-order-companies-and-online-retailers): establish the seller's packaging/manufacturer/producer role and applicable registration, reporting or participation requirements. Check PPWR transition/application dates and national rules, rather than copying an older checklist.
- Additional national duties and product-specific regimes require focused official-source research. A Germany packaging review does not verify all EPR streams, other member states or an actual registration.

### Platform policy

- Amazon [US help](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub) / [Germany help](https://sellercentral.amazon.de/help/hub): research the actual marketplace/category and seller-requested documents. These entry points may require sign-in; use accessible official documents or user-supplied policy text and label access limits.
- [TikTok Shop US Academy](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university): identify the applicable US category/restricted-products, claims and document policies. Do not transfer a policy from another TikTok region without verification.
- [Shopify compliance guidance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/compliance): examine applicable platform terms along with destination-market rules. Shopify's general guidance does not verify a merchant's store or its jurisdiction-specific obligations.

## Interpreting dates and conclusions

Use the user's intended sale period as the main timeline. Ask separately about import, shipment or first placing-on-market dates only when they alter the rule's application. Normalize relative dates using the user's timezone and the actual assessment date; do not silently turn a vague season into a specific day.

Distinguish **applicable now**, **enacted with future application**, **proposal**, and **unresolved status**. Check amendments and transitional provisions for the relevant event and product. A future plan can be assessed against confirmed requirements while remaining explicit about changes not yet knowable. Do not apply a proposed rule as mandatory.

Report a demonstrated issue, missing evidence, not applicable, or not checked as appropriate. Use “not applicable” only with supported applicability reasoning. A researched requirement, a supplied document and a product passing a physical test are different forms of evidence. The final report should describe what was assessed and the remaining actions, never grant categorical permission to sell.
